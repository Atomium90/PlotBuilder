## Self-generated placeholder assets

The scripts named below live in `_Inbox/`, next to the project and outside the repository; only the imported assets are tracked.

**Demolition tool meshes and the shared colour palette** (`_Inbox/Tools`): script-generated by the project's own `generate_tools.py`/`build_fbx.py`, not sourced from a third party. Placeholder art, meant to be replaced by final assets later without touching the gameplay logic (sockets and pivots are the contract, see `_Inbox/Tools/README.md`).

**Caution pole and caution tape meshes** (`_Inbox/Environment`): script-generated by `generate_site_props.py` (same approach and same palette as the tools), not sourced from a third party. The tape is one 100 cm module with the yellow and black stripes built as geometry.

**Interface icons** (`_Inbox/UI_Icons`): the category and tool pictograms of the shop, the inventory and the tool belt are drawn as SVG paths in the project's own `make_icons.py` and rendered to PNG, not sourced from a third party. They stand in until real icons exist.
